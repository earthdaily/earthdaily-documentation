# Landsat

Landsat 8 and 9 imagery at 30m resolution from USGS, providing continuity of the longest-running satellite Earth observation program.

## Available Collections

| Collection Name | Collection Id | Processing Level | Time Range |
|-----------------|---------------|------------------|------------|
| Landsat Collection 2 L1 | `landsat-c2l1` | Top-of-atmosphere radiance | Starting Jan 2013 |
| Landsat Collection 2 L2 Reflectance | `landsat-c2l2-sr` | Surface reflectance | Starting Jan 2013 |
| Landsat Collection 2 L2 Temperature | `landsat-c2l2-st` | Land surface temperature | Starting Jan 2013 |

## Key Characteristics

- **Spatial resolution**: 30m (multispectral), 15m (panchromatic), 100m (thermal)
- **Revisit time**: 8 days with Landsat 8 + 9 combined
- **Swath width**: 185 km
- **Number of bands**: 11 (Landsat 8/9 OLI + TIRS)

## Notes

- Source: AWS Open Data — [USGS Landsat](https://registry.opendata.aws/usgs-landsat/)
