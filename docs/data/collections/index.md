# Data Catalog

Browse the data collections available through EarthDaily. Collections are organized by category to help you find the right data for your application.

## Imagery

Optical and radar satellite imagery from public and commercial sources.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-satellite-variant:{ .lg .middle } __Sentinel-2__

    ---

    Multispectral optical imagery at 10–60m resolution with global coverage every 5 days. Available as L1C, L2A, and Collection 1 L2A.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Learn more](sentinel-2.md)

-   :material-satellite-variant:{ .lg .middle } __Landsat__

    ---

    Landsat 8 and 9 imagery at 30m resolution — surface reflectance, land surface temperature, and top-of-atmosphere radiance.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Learn more](landsat.md)

-   :material-radar:{ .lg .middle } __Sentinel-1__

    ---

    C-band SAR imagery for all-weather, day-and-night monitoring. Available as GRD and radiometrically terrain-corrected (RTC).

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Learn more](sentinel-1.md)

-   :material-satellite-variant:{ .lg .middle } __Venus__

    ---

    High-revisit multispectral imagery from the Venus satellite mission at sub-weekly frequency.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Learn more](venus.md)

-   :material-camera:{ .lg .middle } __EarthDaily Constellation Preview__

    ---

    Preview imagery from the EarthDaily satellite constellation delivering daily global coverage.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Learn more](edc-preview.md)

</div>
