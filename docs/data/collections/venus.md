# Venus

High-revisit multispectral imagery from the CNES/ISA Venus satellite mission, designed for vegetation and environmental monitoring.

## Available Collections

| Collection Name | Collection Id | Processing Level | Time Range |
|-----------------|---------------|------------------|------------|
| Venus L2A | `venus-l2a` | Surface reflectance | Starting Nov 2017 |

## Key Characteristics

- **Spatial resolution**: 5m
- **Revisit time**: 2 days over selected sites
- **Number of bands**: 12 spectral bands (visible to NIR)
- **Coverage**: ~100 predefined sites worldwide
